# MusicLab Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 20, 2026

MusicLab ("the app," "we," "us") is a mobile music-learning and practice-journal app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (handled securely by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password ourselves).

**Practice recordings.** With your permission, MusicLab uses your device's microphone to record you playing so it can analyze your performance against a reference piece (e.g. checking timing and note accuracy) and let you compare recordings over time. These recordings are stored on our backend so you can revisit your practice history.

**Practice and performance data.** We store data about the pieces you practice, your session history, and analysis results (e.g. accuracy scores, streaks) so the app can show your progress over time.

**Purchase information.** If you subscribe to MusicLab Pro, purchase processing is handled by RevenueCat and your app store's payment system. We receive confirmation of your subscription status (e.g. active/expired) but do not receive or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by the app store.

**Device and diagnostic information.** Standard technical information (device type, app version, crash logs) may be collected to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide core app functionality (practice analysis, progress tracking, saving your recordings)
- To manage your account and subscription status
- To fix bugs and improve the app
- We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties
- We do **not** use your practice recordings for advertising or share them publicly without your action (e.g. if you choose to share a recording yourself)

## 3. Third-Party Services

MusicLab relies on the following third-party services to operate:

- **Supabase** — backend database, authentication, and file storage (stores your account info, recordings, and practice data)
- **RevenueCat** — subscription and purchase management

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf.

## 4. Your Choices and Rights

- You can delete individual recordings or practice entries within the app.
- You can request full account deletion, which removes your account and associated data from our systems, by contacting us at the email below.
- You can revoke microphone permission at any time in your device settings; note that this will disable performance analysis and recording features.

## 5. Children's Privacy

MusicLab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including relying on Supabase's security infrastructure. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as MusicLab evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
